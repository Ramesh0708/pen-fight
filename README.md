# Pen Fight

A recess game from the last bench. Your blue ballpoint and Ravi's red one share a lined notebook on a wooden desk. Flick yours. Knock his off the far edge before Madam finishes her round of the corridor.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is nothing to install.

## Rules

- Drag in the direction you want the blue pen to travel, then let go.
- The long sides of the desk bounce. The near and far edges are open.
- Knock Ravi's pen off to score. If yours falls, the point is his.
- If both pens go over, the round is a scratch.
- After eight flicks with nobody on the floor, the pen closer to an edge loses.
- First to 3 takes the desk.

Mouse, trackpad, and touch all work.
